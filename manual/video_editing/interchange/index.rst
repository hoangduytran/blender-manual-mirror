
***********
Interchange
***********

Interchange formats allow video editing timelines to be transferred between
different applications, making it possible to continue editing a project
or collaborate across different video editing workflows.

Unlike rendered video files, interchange formats describe the structure
of an edit, including clip timing, track organization, and references
to source media. This allows the receiving application to reconstruct
the timeline with editable strips.

Not all editing features are supported by every application or interchange
format. Effects, transitions, and application-specific properties may
be interpreted differently or omitted during transfer.
